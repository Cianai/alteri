## Summary of Key Findings

### Social Support Typologies Identified

1. **Tardy (1985) - Five Aspects:**
   - Direction
   - Disposition
   - Description-evaluation
   - Network
   - (Fifth aspect not specified in abstract)

2. **Schonfeld (1991) - Four Functions:**
   - Belonging (social companionship)
   - Appraisal (availability of confidants)
   - Tangible (instrumental support)
   - Self-esteem support

3. **Analytic Technologies/Revista Redes - Six Functions:**
   - Information
   - Social support (emotional/material aid)
   - Companionship
   - Access to resources
   - Sense-making
   - Normative pressures/Influence

4. **NSHAP - Two-Factor Isolation Structure:**
   - Social Disconnectedness Scale:
     - Restricted social network dimension
     - Social inactivity dimension
   - Perceived Isolation Scale:
     - Lack of support
     - Loneliness

### Standard Ego-Network Measures (SAGE Research Methods)

- Size
- Strength
- Diversity
- Centrality
- Constraint
- (Additional: Effective size per Hanneman)

### Name Generator/Interpreter Approaches

- **GSS Approach:** First 5 names only (practical constraint)
- **MGRI (Wisconsin):** Multiple Generator Random Interpreters for support networks
- **NSHAP:** "Important matters" name generator variant
- **Completion rates:** Decline as function of alter count (Cambridge reference)

### Public Datasets with Social Network Modules

1. **NSHAP (National Social Life, Health, and Aging Project)**
   - Archive: ICPSR (studies 20541, 34921, 36873)
   - Data: Core + Social Networks datasets (public/restricted versions)
   - Code: GitLab (https://gitlab.com/nshap)
   - Instruments: Available via NORC PDFs

2. **MIDUS (Midlife in the United States)**
   - Archive: ICPSR + MIDUS Portal (midus.wisc.edu)
   - Variables: ~20,000 across all modules
   - Codebook: Interactive via Colectica Portal

3. **Whitehall II**
   - Method: Berkman-Syme Social Network Index variant (0-28 score)
   - Access: Contact UCL study team
   - Documentation: Embedded in published papers (not standalone codebook)

## Recommendations for CIA-227

1. **Prioritize acquisition of:**
   - Reins et al. (2021) formr tutorial - open-source template
   - Pescosolido et al. (2021) Cambridge volume - comprehensive ego-network methods
   - Vacca chapter (2025) - integration with survey research
   - NSHAP measurement rationale paper (ResearchGate/PMC2763519)

2. **Download NSHAP data:**
   - Register at ICPSR
   - Obtain public-use datasets 1-5 (Core + Social Networks)
   - Review Round 2 codebook for name interpreter structure

3. **Examine existing scales:**
   - ISEL (Interpersonal Support Evaluation List) - excellent psychometrics
   - SSQ (Social Support Questionnaire) - 27 items, availability + satisfaction
   - Schonfeld four-function scales - belonging, appraisal, tangible, self-esteem
   - Berkman-Syme SNI - structural network size/frequency

4. **Methodological considerations:**
   - Limit name interpreters to 5 alters (per GSS precedent) to maintain completion rates
   - Consider MGRI approach for multifaceted support network elicitation
   - Implement two-factor structure (disconnectedness vs perceived isolation) per NSHAP findings
   - Network diversity may predict outcomes better than perceived support (Platt 2013)

5. **Implementation tools:**
   - Explore formr (open-source) for online ego-network data collection
   - Check Vacca's R book for composition analysis code (alter attributes)
   - Use Hanneman textbook for structural measures (effective size, constraint)
