## Literature Searches (Zotero + Web)

### 1. Social Isolation, Loneliness, Network Health Outcomes

**Top Zotero Results:**

1. **Misiak et al. (2026)** - "Unravelling longitudinal associations of social and emotional loneliness with social isolation and mental health outcomes: a cross-lagged panel network analysis"
   - DOI: 10.1017/S2045796025100383
   - Journal: Epidemiology and Psychiatric Sciences, 35, e1
   - **Relevance:** Longitudinal study (N=3275) using De Jong Gierveld 11-item scale, PHQ-9, GAD-7, and Lubben Social Network Scale to differentiate social vs emotional loneliness in network context
   - **Key Finding:** Emotional loneliness showed bidirectional associations with all mental health measures; social loneliness specifically predicted social anxiety and was associated with social isolation

2. **Cacioppo & Cacioppo (2014)** - "Social relationships and health: the toxic effects of perceived social isolation"
   - DOI: 10.1111/spc3.12087
   - Journal: Social and Personality Psychology Compass, 8(2), 58-72
   - **Relevance:** Reviews mechanisms linking loneliness to morbidity/mortality, focusing on perceived vs objective isolation
   - **Key Finding:** Loneliness heightens sensitivity to social threats and impairs executive functioning, sleep, and well-being

3. **Walsh et al. (2025)** - "Why loneliness requires a multidimensional approach: a critical narrative review"
   - Journal: Not specified (status: needs_source)
   - **Relevance:** Argues for multidimensional assessment of loneliness beyond unidimensional scales

4. **Ernst (2025)** - "Loneliness as a transdiagnostic risk factor for mental and physical health in adulthood — state of the evidence, research gaps, and ways forward"
   - **Relevance:** Synthesizes meta-analyses on loneliness as transdiagnostic risk factor

### 2. UCLA Loneliness Scale + Network Structure

**Top Zotero Results:**

1. **Cacioppo et al. (2009)** - "Alone in the crowd: the structure and spread of loneliness in a large social network"
   - DOI: 10.1037/a0016076
   - Journal: Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 97(6), 977-991
   - **Relevance:** Framingham Heart Study data showing loneliness clusters in networks, extends 3 degrees of separation, spreads like contagion
   - **Key Finding:** Loneliness disproportionately represented at network periphery; spread stronger for friends than family, stronger for women than men

2. **van den Bulck et al. (2025)** - "Mapping vulnerability factors of chronic emotional and social loneliness: a network analysis"
   - **Relevance:** Network analysis of vulnerability factors for chronic loneliness dimensions
   - Tags: Attachment styles, Depression, Social anxiety, Chronic loneliness

3. **Widmer et al. (2025)** - "Do Conflict Structures of Family Networks Matter for Loneliness in Later Life? The Case of a Cohort of Swiss Older Adults"
   - **Relevance:** Examines interconnection between loneliness and conflict structures in older adults' family networks

**Web Results:**

## Key Findings Summary

### Validated Scales for Ego-Network Integration:

1. **Loneliness:**
   - UCLA Loneliness Scale (20-item, 3-item short form)
   - De Jong Gierveld Scale (11-item, 6-item short form with emotional/social subscales)

2. **Depression:**
   - PHQ-9 (9-item, cut-off ≥10 for clinical depression, ≥5 for screening)

3. **Well-being:**
   - WHO-5 (5-item, cut-off <51 for depression screening)

4. **Social Networks:**
   - Lubben Social Network Scale (6-item)
   - Social Network Index (SNI) from ELSA/HRS (4 dimensions)

### Digital Implementation Evidence:

- **PHQ-9:** Multiple electronic implementations with automated scoring; used in EMA studies
- **WHO-5:** Free, automated scoring (×4 conversion), PsyToolkit template available
- **De Jong Gierveld:** Manual available; emotional/social subscale scoring documented
- **UCLA:** Short 3-item version validated for large surveys

### Dataset Resources:

- **Add Health:** Social network modules available; both public and restricted access
- **ELSA:** Waves 0-11 via UK Data Service; includes Social Network Index
- **SHARE:** Part of 4-study harmonization project; cross-national social network data

### Research Gaps Identified:

1. Limited GitHub repositories for open-source digital implementations
2. Need for integration guides combining ego-network elicitation with health outcome measures
3. Validation of combined instruments (network + health) in single digital platform
