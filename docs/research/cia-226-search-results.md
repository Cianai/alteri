## Key Findings Summary

### Methodological Approaches

1. **Longitudinal Network Models:**
   - MRQAP (Multiple Regression Quadratic Assignment Procedure)
   - STERGM (Separable Temporal Exponential Random Graph Models)
   - SAOM/RSiena (Stochastic Actor Oriented Models) - most established for panel data
   - Multilevel Latent Growth Models for alter trajectories

2. **Alter Trajectory Typology (Offer et al. 2026):**
   - Continuously active
   - Awakened (dormant → active)
   - Dormant (active → dormant)
   - Dropped (active → gone)
   - Transitory (new → dropped quickly)
   - New (recently added)

3. **EMA Integration Approaches:**
   - Interaction-based elicitation embedded in EMA (Semborski et al. 2023)
   - Personal social network data + experience sampling (Stadel et al. 2024)
   - Feasibility studies combining EMA + SNA (Prochnow et al. 2025)

### Software Ecosystem

**R Packages:**
- `RSiena`: Longitudinal network panel analysis (SAOM)
- `egor`: Ego-network data management
- `netdiffuseR`: Network diffusion over time

**Mobile Platforms:**
- DOSE (watchOS): Apple Watch EMA framework
- EMI-Linebot: LINE-based EMA
- Student-Life: Android passive sensing + EMA

**Open Datasets:**
- GLOBEM: ~500 users, multi-year, weekly EMA
- Student-Life: 48 students, 10 weeks, passive + EMA
- EmotionTimeSeries: Seven open ESM datasets

### Design Considerations

1. **Panel Conditioning Bias:** Repeated measurement may alter behavior (Warren & Halpern-Manners 2012)
2. **Recall Bias:** Traditional elicitation vs. interaction-based real-time reporting
3. **Alter Ordering:** Most important/frequent alters named first, trend downward (Semborski et al. 2023)
4. **Compliance:** Need for best practices to reduce EMA burden (Businelle et al. 2024)
5. **Network Boundaries:** Size and density affect tie dormancy patterns (Marin 2019)

### Research Gaps

1. Limited integration of EMA with full ego-network elicitation (most focus on specific alters or interactions)
2. Few studies tracking complete network evolution (new, active, dormant, dropped) in real-time
3. Tension between comprehensive network measurement and EMA participant burden
4. Need for validated measures distinguishing true network change from measurement artifact
